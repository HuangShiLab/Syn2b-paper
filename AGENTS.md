# Syn2b paper-writing policy

For any manuscript-writing, editing, polishing, review, response-to-reviewers, abstract,
introduction, discussion, figure-caption, or submission-readiness task in this repository:

1. Consult and apply the relevant `nature-*` skills, especially `nature-writing`,
   `nature-polishing`, `nature-response`, `nature-reviewer`, `nature-figure`,
   and `nature-statistics` when applicable.
2. Also apply the installed anti-defensive-writing skill:
   - `anti-defensive-writing-en` for English manuscript text;
   - `anti-defensive-writing` for Chinese drafting or discussion.
3. Build the narrative around the strongest evidence-supported contribution.
4. Do not add defensive-only caveats, self-undermining wording, or unnecessary limitations.
5. Keep only limitations that are required to define the scope of a claim or that are
   directly supported by results.
6. Before finalizing any factual claim, verify it against the repository results or
   named source files.
